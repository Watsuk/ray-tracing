# Code of Conduct

## Guidelines

This project is dedicated to providing a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for all participants. 

We follow the standards and principles defined in the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

### Expectations

- Be respectful and considerate of different viewpoints and backgrounds.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, or unwelcome conduct are not tolerated.

### Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior or violations, please contact the maintainers at `jakarta.pia@absimilia.tech`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
